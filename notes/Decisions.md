# Decisions (one line each, newest last)

- 2026-11-15: Rojo 7.4.4 + Selene + StyLua + Lune, pinned in rokit.toml. Pure modules tested in Lune; feel tested in Studio by the DEV panel.
- 2026-11-15: Gravity set to 120 (Roblox default 196.2) so ramps give real air time and flips. Spec numbers (top speed, timeToTop, drop) are kept; tune in Studio.
- 2026-11-15: The car controller runs once per frame at PreSimulation (not 240 Hz) with a 1/30 s step cap; it is stable at 30 and 60 fps in the drop simulation.
- 2026-11-15: Wheel forces are summed into one VectorForce at the centre of mass plus one Torque (r×F), instead of one VectorForce per wheel. Same physics, fewer constraints.
- 2026-11-15: The damper is a velocity-change impulse (progressive above 15 studs/s, 1.5× on rebound, ×6 at the bump stop) with a one-frame lookahead ray. A force damper bounced 5 studs and was unstable at 30 fps.
- 2026-11-15: Players have no avatar (CharacterAutoLoads off). The rider is a client-built 6-piece rig; others see it through the Vehicle.Pose stream (10 Hz, 7 or 127 bytes).
- 2026-11-15: Ram test thresholds use closing speed × mass factor; static walls count as mass factor 2.0. Test speeds 6, 15 and 35 studs/s give Bump, HardHit and HugeCrash.
- 2026-11-15: Milestone 1 lets every vehicle be tried on the test track; ownership checks come with saves (milestone 2).
- 2026-11-15: Vibration works on gamepads only for now; phone vibration waits until the haptics API is checked on a real phone (verify).
- 2026-11-15 (1b): Damage spill goes front/rear to left+right, left/right to front+rear, roof to left+right (25% each). Wheel share is split between the two wheels on that end or side by where the hit landed.
- 2026-11-15 (1b): Landing wheel damage = max(0, fall speed - 50) x 1.2 to each wheel that touched down. Using the full impact formula would pop all four wheels off a 20-stud drop (fall speed 69 at gravity 120).
- 2026-11-15 (1b): Every look, detach and glass break is derived from the 9 zone numbers, so every device draws the same wreck from Damage.State. Glass cracks when its zone turns dented (same as the spec's "hit over I 30" for single hits).
- 2026-11-15 (1b): In looks-only modes (quick races) the engine keeps running at 0 front health, so damage never changes handling; it only smokes.
- 2026-11-15 (1b): Explosion shockwaves are applied by each owner to their own car when they see another car's exploded flag (the owner always moves its own car). Brawl's server-applied version comes in milestone 7.
- 2026-11-15 (1b): The crash replay never moves the real car (its position replicates to everyone). The real car is held still and hidden locally while a local copy plays back. It starts 0.3 s after the crash so the rider's flight is in it, and runs only when the rider was thrown. Other players' cars keep moving live during it.
- 2026-11-15 (1b): Mesh deformation uses the body shell part (Body_Shell, or Panel_Floor on the blockouts). Device memory cannot be read from a game script, so the benchmark uses fps plus the player's own manual graphics quality (under 4 = off).
- 2026-11-15 (1b): Damage.State server limit is 5 per s, burst 6 (clients send at most 4) so explosion and repair sends never count toward the kick limit. Owners resend every 3 s while damaged, which also covers late joiners.
- 2026-11-15 (1b): The HUD shows the damage silhouette on the test track too (the spec puts it in Brawl and Highway), so it can be checked now.
- 2026-11-15 (2): Profile v1 additions (no spec field changed): tracks = { [mapId] = { best ms, stars, splits } }, ghosts and ghostOrder (best-run ghosts; starter maps always kept, newest 20 others), flags.boardBanDay (3 rejected runs in 10 min).
- 2026-11-15 (2): The DataStore value is { data = profile, lock = { jobId, time } }. A join waits 4 x 5 s for another server's lock, takes over one older than 30 min, else asks the player to rejoin. If DataStores are down at join, the player plays on a fresh profile that is never saved and is told so. Studio without API access keeps profiles in memory.
- 2026-11-15 (2): Run times are the server's clock from Run.Start to Run.Finish (spec: client time is advisory; it is only logged when over 1 s off). Checkpoint and finish reports count only with the car within 80 studs of them on the server.
- 2026-11-15 (2): Speed checks follow the spec's two rules: a 4 Hz step over top speed x 1.4 + 90 studs/s sends the car back to its last checkpoint and flags (5 a session kicks), and ground covered over any 1 s window above top speed x 1.25 + the speed pad boost (horizontal only, so falls never count) rejects the finish.
- 2026-11-15 (2): Onboarding funnel steps follow the spec list: 1 Joined, 2 First throttle, 3 Jump (counted at Pip's Hill checkpoint 1, at the end of the ramp's landing zone), 4 Finish Pip's Hill, 5 Second track. Later steps (free paint, shop, hub, Highway, Brawl) come with their milestones.
- 2026-11-15 (2): Trail coins are picked up on the client and the count is sent with Run.Finish; the server clamps it to the map's coin count (13 at most on a starter map, inside the daily cap).
- 2026-11-15 (2): The speed check lets through a jump that lands within 25 studs of a respawn point (start, checkpoints, hazard respawns), so resets are never flagged.
- 2026-11-15 (2): In Tracks every chassis is in the Ghost collision group: cars pass through each other and wheel raycasts use the chassis group so they ignore other cars. Other players' cars and riders show at 60% opacity, like your own ghost.
- 2026-11-15 (2): Pip's Hill's spinner is a full-width foam roller with a guaranteed-hit volume (the beat sheet's "hit is guaranteed") and respawn just past it; Tumble Gulch's is a sweeping bar checked on the client against the car, a real dodge with the normal checkpoint respawn.
- 2026-11-15 (2): Each map resets a falling car 30 studs below its lowest piece; a decorative ground sits 45 studs below so a falling car never lands on it.
- 2026-11-15 (2): First-time players spawn straight onto Pip's Hill; returning players start on the first starter map without Gold. A MAPS picker stands in for the hub (milestone 6). The test track stays for the DEV tests only.
- 2026-11-15 (2): Pip's Hill's first finish pays exactly 100 coins and 1 star (no boost) instead of the normal pay, once. Later finishes pay normally.
- 2026-11-15 (2): Results lock for 2 s, then NEXT counts down 5 s and goes by itself. RETRY resets the car to the start line locally (no new car), so it is instant.
- 2026-11-15 (2): Settings use choice buttons and -/+ steppers only (nothing typed). They save at most once per 5 s; server values never overwrite a change not yet sent.
- 2026-11-15 (2): In Tracks the damage silhouette shows only once the car is damaged (less clutter on a clean run).
- 2026-11-15 (2): Milestone list corrected to the spec's numbering (2 Core loop ... 9 Polish and launch); quick races move to milestone 6 with the round manager.
- 2026-11-15 (3): Builder part costs are the parts our pieces really make (placeholder art): checkpoint 4, finish 5, curve 45 is 2 road segments and curve 90 is 3, bouncer and speed pad 2 (pad + trigger), spinner 2, hammer 5 (posts, frame, arm, head), hazard bar 3 (posts + bar). The spec's lower numbers assume single-mesh prefabs from the art pass; the 400-part limit counts real parts either way.
- 2026-11-15 (3): MapCodec header is 8 bytes: version, flags (bit 0 full damage, bits 1-2 theme), tags (one bit each), the 3 name word ids, piece count. Pieces are the spec's 11 bytes; rotation byte = yaw step (0..23) + 24 x tilt (0 none, 1-3 quarter turns on X, 4-6 on Z). 250 pieces = 2,758 bytes.
- 2026-11-15 (3): Grid units are 2 studs; the normal grid snaps every 2 units (4 studs, spec) and VIP's fine grid every unit (2 studs). Height is whole studs, 0 to 256. Map space is centred on the plot.
- 2026-11-15 (3): Road pieces are anchored at the middle of their entry edge and run forward, so a piece placed at another's end joins it; the builder snaps a road piece dropped within 12 studs of a road end onto it. Curves turn left with a 48-stud radius so a 90 degree curve ends on the grid (drive it backwards for a right turn).
- 2026-11-15 (3): Tilted blocks, walls and platforms turn about their centre and then sit back on their floor; a tilted platform becomes a slab.
- 2026-11-15 (3): Checkpoints count in placement order, and builder maps show each checkpoint's number on its arch.
- 2026-11-15 (3): Moving pieces animate on each client from the server clock, and their hits are tested on the client from the map data (the same box the milestone 2 sweeping bar used). The piston shoves the car (45 studs/s sideways, 10 up) only while it punches out; the tilt deck is a solid deck each client rocks locally.
- 2026-11-15 (3): MapBuilder moved to Shared so the builder preview on the device is built by the same code as the server's maps. The preview is client-only: TEST drives on it, and the server checks the run against the same map definition (checkpoint and finish positions, respawn points, speed checks).
- 2026-11-15 (3): TEST from the start gate is a normal server-timed run on a test map; a finish stores the tested pieces' fingerprint and creator time for the session only (a rejoin means testing again). Changing the name, tags or full damage after a TEST does not need a new TEST; changing any piece does. Practice from a checkpoint never reaches the server. Test-map rejects never count toward the leaderboard ban.
- 2026-11-15 (3): A map must take at least 5 s (the spec's minimum time floor), so a TEST finish under 5 s does not count.
- 2026-11-15 (3): Profile additions: maps.drafts keys are slot strings ("1".."15") holding base64 MapCodec buffers; maps.links[slot] = the published mapId (a republish updates that map, keeping plays and ratings); maps.lastPublish (the cooldown survives a rejoin); stats.playSeconds (the 15-minute gate, added at every save).
- 2026-11-15 (3): Plots are 1,300 studs apart along +Z from (0, 0, 6000), 16 per server, away from the starter maps.
- 2026-11-15 (3): Map record (Maps_v1, M_<mapId>): v, owner, name {adjective, noun, place, number}, tags, flags, data (base64), creatorMs, created, updated, plays, ratingSum, ratingCount, hidden, boardEpoch. The name number comes from an N_<a>_<n>_<p> counter. A republish that moves the creator time over 10% bumps boardEpoch (the leaderboard reset, read in milestone 4). New maps also go in MapIndex_v1 (scope Newest) for browse.
- 2026-11-15 (3): A publish blocked only by the cooldown is queued on the server and goes out when the cooldown ends (Publish when ready), unless the player leaves.
- 2026-11-15 (3): In Studio the play-time gate is 1 minute and the cooldown 1 minute, so nate can try every gate in one sitting; live servers use 15 minutes and 10 minutes.
- 2026-11-15 (3): New map names start from RANDOM words (fun, and one tap); the wheels change them. Without VIP the slot picker shows the 7 VIP slots locked with one line on what VIP adds (no purchase prompt until milestone 5).
- 2026-11-15 (4): Published maps load into one of 24 map slots per server, 1,300 studs apart along -Z from (0, 0, -6000), away from the starter maps (+X) and build plots (+Z). The server builds 40 pieces a frame and parents the model when whole; a map nobody has been on for 60 s is unloaded (with all 24 slots used, the one empty longest is reused).
- 2026-11-15 (4): A play is a run started by someone other than the creator, once per player per map per day (profile daily.mapStarts). It adds to plays and playsByDay (last 7 days) and pays the creator 5 coins, at most 200 a day per creator, tracked in Maps_v1 R_<userId> so the cap holds across servers. Royalties are paid when the creator is in a server (at once if they are in this one).
- 2026-11-15 (4): Plays, finishers and ratings are batched per map on each server and written every minute; the Popular index is rewritten every 10 minutes for maps that changed. Popular score = plays in 7 days x (average/5)^2 x 100; under 10 ratings a map counts as 3.5 stars and its card says NEW.
- 2026-11-15 (4): Ratings need a finish on that map and never your own; a player can change theirs (the sum moves, the count does not). Profile tracks and ratings for player maps are kept for the newest 500 maps (trackOrder; the spec keeps the last 500 ratings); starter maps are never trimmed.
- 2026-11-15 (4): Reports: 3 preset reasons only. Every report gets the same thanks; it counts only from accounts 3+ days old that finished the map, once per account, never your own. Hide at 5 counted reports or over 10% of finishers once 20 have finished.
- 2026-11-15 (4): Every hide (by reports or an admin) is a creator strike; 3 strikes in 30 days bans publishing for 7 days, then 30 days, then for good. A ban clears the strikes. Bans live in Moderation_v1 U_<userId> (read fresh on every publish and copied to the profile on join).
- 2026-11-15 (4): Featured is hand-picked by admins from the browse card's gear (FEATURE, HIDE, UNHIDE, bans). This replaces a bot reachability check: every published map was already finished by its creator in TEST, and admins look before featuring. Admins also get a REVIEW tab of hidden maps.
- 2026-11-15 (4): Card thumbnails are sketches drawn on the device from the map's own pieces (no image rendering, no storage); big maps draw every nth piece, at most 160 frames a card.
- 2026-11-15 (4): The STARTER tab replaces the milestone 2 map picker. NEXT on a player map drives the next card from the list it was picked from, then opens browse.
- 2026-11-15 (4): Deferred: per-map leaderboards to milestone 6 (records keep boardEpoch for them), Sponsored browse slots to Update 2 (nate's spec decision). Full damage needs no change for free runs (Tracks already uses full damage); quick races in milestone 6 read the flag.
